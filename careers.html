Explore Possibilities

Many Ways to Build a Future

Career pathways can include further education, technical and vocational training, apprenticeships, public service, entrepreneurship and employment. The right next step depends on a student's interests, qualifications and available opportunities.

🎓

Further Education

Research entry requirements, application dates, costs and course options for institutions you may wish to attend.

🧰

Technical and Vocational Skills

Explore recognised training options that build practical abilities for specific trades, services and industries.

💼

Employment and Public Service

Learn about workplace expectations, professional conduct, communication, teamwork and the qualifications required for roles of interest.

🚀

Entrepreneurship

Develop problem-solving, planning, budgeting and customer-service skills that can support responsible business ideas.

Plan Step by Step

Turn Interests into Practical Goals

Start by identifying subjects you enjoy, skills you want to strengthen and the kinds of problems you would like to solve. Then compare those interests with the entry requirements and costs of possible study or training options.

Speak with teachers, family members and appropriate career or training advisers. Verify information with the relevant institution or employer before making financial or enrolment decisions.

Career Planning Checklist

  • Identify interests, strengths and areas for improvement.
  • Research qualifications and entry requirements.
  • Check application deadlines and required documents.
  • Compare costs, location and support options.
  • Keep schoolwork, attendance and assessments on track.
  • Ask a trusted teacher or adviser for guidance.

Be Prepared

Build Skills Employers and Institutions Value

Clear communication, literacy, numeracy, reliability, digital awareness, teamwork, respectful conduct and a willingness to learn can help students prepare for many different pathways. Keep records of achievements and activities where appropriate, and present your experience honestly.

Verify Opportunities

This page provides general guidance and does not announce a current scholarship, vacancy, training placement or recruitment opportunity. Check official notices and contact the relevant institution directly for current details.
