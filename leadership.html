Principal & School Administration

Providing Direction and Coordination

The Principal and administration are central points of leadership and official communication for the school. Their responsibilities generally include coordinating school operations, supporting teaching and learning, communicating school requirements and working with relevant education authorities, families and community stakeholders.

For Busu Secondary School’s current leadership arrangements, please contact the school office. This page does not name an individual because a current, formally verified leadership list has not been supplied for publication.

Leadership Responsibilities

Working Together Across the School

Strong leadership is supported by clear roles, professional cooperation and a shared focus on students’ learning and wellbeing.

🧭

Direction & Planning

Set school priorities, communicate expectations and coordinate efforts to support learning and responsible school operations.

📚

Teaching & Learning

Support staff and students in maintaining purposeful classrooms, academic progress and a culture of continuous improvement.

🤝

Community & Accountability

Encourage appropriate communication with parents, guardians, staff, education partners and the wider community.
