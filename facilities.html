Responsible Use

Looking After School Resources

Facilities remain useful when they are used safely, respectfully and in accordance with school rules.

Everyone Has a Role

Respect, Safety and Maintenance

  • Follow instructions from teachers and authorised staff when using school facilities or equipment.
  • Use classrooms, learning materials and shared spaces for their intended purposes.
  • Keep the campus clean and dispose of rubbish responsibly.
  • Report damaged equipment, hazards or maintenance needs to a teacher or the school administration.
  • Do not enter restricted areas or use specialist equipment without permission and supervision.
