Who We Are

A School Community Focused on Learning

Busu Secondary School serves students and families in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. This website is designed to help the school share reliable information and strengthen communication with its community.

Our School

Creating Opportunities Through Education

Secondary education helps young people develop knowledge, skills, confidence and the ability to make informed choices. At Busu Secondary School, the school community works toward a learning environment where students can grow academically, socially and personally.

Students, teachers, school leadership, parents, guardians and community partners each have an important role in supporting positive learning and student development.

This page provides a general introduction. Official statements about the school’s history, enrolment, curriculum, facilities and current priorities should be confirmed and updated by the school administration.

A Distinctive Learning Feature

Agriculture, Nature & Learning by Doing

Agriculture can connect classroom learning with everyday life by introducing concepts such as plant growth, soil, food production, environmental care and sustainable use of resources.

Busu Secondary School’s website highlights an agriculture and wildlife learning feature as a distinctive part of its school story. The dedicated page can share school-confirmed information about practical activities, student projects and environmental learning, including any gardens, livestock, fishponds or wildlife initiatives that the school verifies.
